APEX — Anticipatory Predictive Safety-Constrained Planner

A safety-constrained predictive optimizer that inherits the best of three foundational driving models, enhanced as a learning-augmented MPC. VinUni / Ocean Park motorcycle-interaction study.

1 · Inheritance — best of three foundational models

LONGITUDINAL

IDM

Intelligent Driver Model · Treiber et al. 2000
Crash-free safe time-headway & disciplined braking.
→ APEX inherits this as a hard clearance margin + a stopping-distance yield cap.
REACTIVE

ORCA / Velocity Obstacle

van den Berg et al. 2011 · Fiorini&Shiller 1998
Collision avoidance against moving agents.
→ APEX inherits this as a multi-obstacle predicted-occupancy constraint over the horizon.
TRAJECTORY

Frenet / Werling + moto-aware

Werling et al. 2010 · this project
Smooth optimal trajectories & lateral-intent anticipation.
→ APEX inherits this as multi-horizon sampling + defensive prediction.

The conservative baseline contributes the philosophy — “always keep a guaranteed safe margin” — but APEX achieves it without crawling (it maximizes speed subject to safety, rather than driving slow everywhere).

2 · The APEX core — a safety-constrained optimizer

Receding-horizon, every 0.1 s

Each step solves: maximize progress subject to a hard collision-margin constraint — min cost(v,d) s.t. clearance ≥ margin ∀ motos, ∀ t∈horizon
1

Multi-horizon sampling

Quintic lateral × quartic longitudinal, horizons T∈{1.5,2.5,4.0}s.

fixes: lets it brake hard fast yet cruise when clear
2

Defensive prediction

Each moto = const-velocity + a reachability envelope; adjacent leaders treated as potential intruders.

fixes: baseline’s constant-position blind spot
3

Hard footprint margin

Separating-axis box clearance to every moto must stay ≥ margin over the whole horizon.

fixes: crude point-box check → collision-free by construction
4

Junction-yield

Near-stationary roadside moto ⇒ stopping-distance speed-cap; per-moto “entered” flag releases it after the cross.

fixes: perpendicular crossings (0/4) that beat everyone else
5

Constrained-optimal pick

Among feasible: min (v0−v1)²+0.6 d1²; if none, brace (max clearance).

fixes: over-braking of IDM / conservative

Literal domination on every cell is impossible — clearance and efficiency are opposed (conservative “wins” clearance only by driving slowly).

4 · Bonus — learning-augmented MPC enhancement

Today APEX has no learned components and assumes perfect perception, scripted behaviour, and hand-tuned uncertainty. The enhancement replaces those three fakes — keeping APEX as a verifiable safety shield while learning lives in perception, prediction, and calibration.

PERCEPTION & PREDICTION FRONT-END (learned)

📹 YOLO detect

Motorcycles in the real dashcam video (already in repo).

▸

🔗 Track

ByteTrack/DeepSORT → stable IDs, per-track velocity.

▸

🗺 BEV lift

IPM + camera intrinsics + GPS/map → road-frame (s,d).

▸

📈 EKF

State + covariance Σ: gives d_d and real uncertainty.

▸

🧠 Behaviour predictor

Trained on tracked trajectories → multimodal future + cut-in intent.

WHAT GETS LEARNED
  • VN behaviour predictor — self-supervised on YOLO tracks (future of a track = its own label). Outputs P(cut-in) + trajectory distribution.
  • Ego dynamics — IMU/GPS/OBD calibrate a_max, jerk, comfort (replace guessed constants).
  • Cost weights — inverse RL / behaviour cloning from the human driver’s demonstrations.
WHAT CHANGES IN APEX
  • Uncertainty inflation (k_unc, reach) → driven by measured Σ + intent prob.
  • Hard margin → chance constraint P(collision) < ε over predicted distribution.
  • Cost weights → calibrated, not hand-tuned.
  • APEX itself stays a training-free, interpretable safety shield over the learned predictor.
CLOSED LOOP (your project goal)

Real trips → YOLO + tracking → (i) moto-trajectory dataset (train predictor) + (ii) ego sensor logs (calibrate dynamics & driver style) → plug calibrated behaviour + ego model back into the simulator → the benchmark now runs on real VN behaviour distributions, not scripted smoothsteps → optional RL refinement with APEX as the shield.
